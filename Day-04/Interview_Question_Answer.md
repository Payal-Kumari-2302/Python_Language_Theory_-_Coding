Day-04 — Python Operators Interview Questions
1. What is an operator in Python?

An operator is a symbol used to perform an operation on values or variables.

2. What are the different types of operators in Python?

The main types are Arithmetic, Comparison, Logical, Assignment, Membership, Identity and Bitwise operators.

3. What are arithmetic operators?

Arithmetic operators are used to perform mathematical operations.

Examples: +, -, *, /, %, //, **

4. What is the difference between / and //?

/ performs normal division, while // performs floor division.

5. What is the use of the % operator?

The % operator returns the remainder after division.

6. What is the use of the ** operator?

The ** operator is used to calculate the power of a number.

7. What are comparison operators?

Comparison operators compare two values and return True or False.

Examples: >, <, >=, <=, ==, !=

8. What is the difference between = and ==?

= is used for assignment, while == is used to compare two values.

9. What are logical operators in Python?

The logical operators are and, or and not.

10. What does the and operator do?

It returns True when both conditions are true.

11. What does the or operator do?

It returns True when at least one condition is true.

12. What does the not operator do?

It reverses the Boolean result.

13. What are assignment operators?

Assignment operators are used to assign or update values.

Examples: =, +=, -=, *=, /=

14. What is the use of the += operator?

It adds a value to a variable and assigns the updated value back to the variable.

Example:

x = 10
x += 5

Here, x becomes 15.

15. What are membership operators?

in and not in are membership operators used to check whether a value exists in a sequence or collection.

16. What are identity operators?

is and is not are identity operators used to check whether two variables refer to the same object.

17. What is the difference between == and is?

== compares values, while is checks whether two variables refer to the same object.

18. What are bitwise operators?

Bitwise operators perform operations on the binary representation of numbers.

Examples: &, |, ^, ~, <<, >>

19. What is operator precedence?

Operator precedence determines the order in which operators are evaluated in an expression.
